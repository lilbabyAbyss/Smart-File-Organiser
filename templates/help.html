How It Works

1

Upload Your Files

Drag & drop files into the upload box or click to select them.

2

Click “Organise Files”

The organiser automatically detects file types and sorts them.

3

Wait for Processing

You may see a confirmation step depending on settings.

4

Download Your ZIP

Files are grouped into folders like TextFiles, PythonFiles, and OtherFiles.

Frequently Asked Questions

What file types are supported?

Most common formats like .txt, .py, .pdf, .jpg, .docx, etc.

Can I undo the organisation?

Yes — original uploads are not modified, only copied into a ZIP.

Does it organise subfolders?

No — only uploaded files are processed.

Is my data safe?

Files are processed locally on your system and not uploaded anywhere else.

Common Issues

“Nothing happens when I click Organise Files”

Make sure your Flask server is running and refresh the page.

“The ZIP is empty”

No valid files were uploaded or all files failed validation.

“Some files didn’t sort correctly”

Unsupported or extensionless files go into OtherFiles.
