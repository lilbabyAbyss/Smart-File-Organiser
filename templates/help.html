How It Works

1

Upload Your Files

Drag & drop files into the upload box or click to select them.

2

Click “Organise Files”

The organiser automatically detects file types and sorts them.

3

Wait for Processing

You may see a confirmation box if multiple files are being sorted.

4

Download Your ZIP

Your sorted files are returned as a ZIP containing folders like TextFiles, PythonFiles, and OtherFiles.

Frequently Asked Questions

What file types are supported?

All common file types including .txt, .py, .pdf, .jpg, .docx, .zip and more.

Can I undo the organisation?

Yes — your original files are never modified. You simply receive a ZIP copy.

Does it organise subfolders?

No — only the files you upload are sorted. Subfolder scanning is not used.

Is my data safe?

All processing happens locally on your machine. No files are uploaded to any server.

Common Issues

“Nothing happens when I click Organise Files”

Ensure your Flask server is running and refresh the page.

“The ZIP is empty”

Make sure you uploaded at least one file before submitting.

“Some files didn’t sort correctly”

They may be unsupported or have no extension. These go into OtherFiles.
